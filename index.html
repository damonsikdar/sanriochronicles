Welcome to Sanrio Chronicles

Where your favorite Sanrio characters go to school, make friends, and create memories that last forever.

The Universe of Sanrio Chronicles

Sanrio Chronicles is a kawaii universe like no other — where your favorite Sanrio characters live out their high school years full of friendship, rivalry, heartbreak, and laughter. Ten main students walk the halls of Sanrio High, each carrying their own dreams, secrets, and wounds. On the surface it looks like the sweetest school in the world. But underneath the pastel walls and cherry blossoms, something much deeper is unfolding.

High School Life

Classes, clubs, cafeteria drama, first loves, and friend group fallouts. The 10 main characters navigate the everyday chaos of being a teenager — the joy of a good day and the weight of a bad one — all wrapped in the most kawaii school universe imaginable.

The Metaverse Realm

Beyond the hallways of Sanrio High lies a secret — a video game-like parallel world that pulls the characters in without warning. Each realm has its own rules, its own dangers, and its own version of the truth. Not everyone makes it out the same way they went in.

Traumatic Entities

Dark forces exist in this universe — entities that feed on emotion, memory, and fear. They don't look like monsters. Sometimes they look like people you trust. The characters must face them together, even when falling apart feels easier.

Deep Emotions

Sanrio Chronicles doesn't shy away from the hard stuff. Heartbreak, grief, identity, loneliness — every character carries something real. The kawaii world doesn't erase the pain. It holds space for it, and that's what makes it beautiful.

Same school. Same friends. A universe that goes deeper than you ever expected.
