The Characters of Sanrio High

Get to know everyone at the most kawaii school in the world.

Hello Kitty

New Student · The Heart of the Group

Hello Kitty just transferred to Sanrio High alongside her best friends Dear Daniel and My Sweet Piano, and adjusting hasn't been easy. Making new friends is a struggle — she's got a temper that flares and a roar that surprises people. But underneath all of that is one of the kindest hearts you'll ever find. She's the glue that holds her whole crew together, even when she doesn't realize it.

  • Best friends: Dear Daniel, My Sweet Piano
  • Favorite subject: Math Batsu
  • Favorite colors: Red, blue & white
  • Crush: Pochacco (soccer team captain)
  • Favorite food: Double cheeseburger with salted spicy fries
  • Favorite drink: Frappuccino

My Melody

Baking Club President · Year 3

Sweet, gentle, and always baking something delicious. My Melody fills the school with the smell of fresh pastries and warm smiles every single day.

  • Club: Baking Club
  • Favorite subject: Home Economics
  • Best friend: Hello Kitty
🐶

Cinnamoroll

Art Club Star · Year 2

Cinnamoroll is dreamy, quiet, and loves floating on clouds — literally. His art pieces always end up on the school bulletin board, and he blushes every single time.

  • Club: Art Club
  • Favorite subject: Art
  • Best friend: Pompompurin
🖤

Kuromi

Drama Club Lead · Year 3

Kuromi may act tough, but she secretly writes the most heartfelt diary entries and has a soft spot for cute things she'd never admit to. The stage is her kingdom.

  • Club: Drama Club
  • Favorite subject: Theatre
  • Best friend: My Melody (rivalry turned friendship)
🍮

Pompompurin

Sports Team Captain · Year 2

Laid-back and lovable, Pompompurin somehow always ends up napping at the exact moment he's needed — yet always shows up for his team when it counts.

  • Club: Track & Field
  • Favorite subject: P.E.
  • Best friend: Cinnamoroll
🐸

Keroppi

Science Club Star · Year 1

Energetic and curious, Keroppi is the first one to raise his hand in class and the last one to leave the science lab. His enthusiasm is completely contagious.

  • Club: Science Club
  • Favorite subject: Biology
  • Best friend: Everyone
