Hello Kitty

New Transfer Student · The Heart of the Group

Hello Kitty just transferred to Sanrio High alongside her lifelong best friends My Sweet Piano and Dear Daniel. Adjusting hasn't been easy — she's got a temper that flares and a roar that surprises people. But underneath all of that is one of the kindest hearts you'll ever find. She's the glue that holds her whole crew together, even when she doesn't realize it.

  • Best friends: My Sweet Piano, Dear Daniel
  • Love story: Started with a crush on Pochacco — slowly realized her true feelings were for Dear Daniel all along
  • Personality: Fiery, loyal, surprisingly tender-hearted
  • Favorite subject: Math Batsu

My Sweet Piano

Hello Kitty's Best Friend · The Energetic One

My Sweet Piano has been Hello Kitty's best friend since first grade. She is endlessly cheerful, talks to almost everyone she meets, and can get excited over almost anything. She had a habit of making friends within minutes, even if she had just met someone. And yes — she will forget her water bottle every single day. She's dating Buppi, who she had a huge crush on long before they got together — a crush that only grew after he gave her his own jacket when she was cold.

  • Best friend: Hello Kitty
  • Dating: Buppi
  • Known for: Making friends within minutes of meeting someone
  • Personality: Energetic, crafty, warm, a little chaotic
  • Favorite subject: Art

Dear Daniel

Hello Kitty's Best Friend · The Calm One

Dear Daniel has been Hello Kitty's best friend since middle school. He's calm, easygoing, and usually the one keeping everyone on schedule. Unlike Piano, Daniel never tries too hard to impress anyone. He showed up on the first day of Sanrio High in his baby blue compact car, ready to drive the group to wherever the story was headed.

  • Best friend: Hello Kitty
  • Known for: Always the calm one keeping the group on schedule
  • Personality: Calm, dependable, charming, shy
  • Favorite subject: Math Batsu

Pochacco

Soccer Team Captain · School Heartthrob

Pochacco is the chill athletic heartthrob of Sanrio High — the kind of person everyone notices the moment he walks into a room. He's My Melody's ex, which makes things complicated now that Hello Kitty is on the scene. Hello Kitty caught a glimpse of him on her first day and couldn't look away. He's interested in her too. My Melody noticed.

  • Circle: Badtz-Maru, Keroppi, Pompompurin
  • Known for: School heartthrob and soccer team captain — everyone notices when he walks in
  • Personality: Chill, athletic, quietly charming
  • Favorite subject: All Sports

Badtz-Maru

Senior · Pochacco's Right-Hand Man

Badtz-Maru is one of the coolest guys at Sanrio High — confident, funny, and effortlessly popular without even trying. He's sassy, witty, and sometimes completely chaotic, always ready with a sarcastic comment before you even finish talking. Don't let the attitude fool you though. The moment someone outside the group gives his friends a hard time, he's the first one to step forward. He acts tough, but he's fiercely loyal.

  • Circle: Pochacco's right-hand man ("Bro #2") — with Keroppi & Pompompurin
  • Dating: Kuromi — regulars at the Haunted Diner, constantly roasting each other
  • Personality: Sassy, witty, laid-back, fiercely loyal
  • Favorite subject: All Sports

My Melody

School Queen Bee · Pochacco's Ex

My Melody is the queen bee of Sanrio High — polished, popular, and not used to losing anything she considers hers. She broke up with Pochacco, but she definitely noticed when he started paying attention to the new transfer student. Her response to finding Hello Kitty's open locker on the first day? A quiet "Oh...nothing." To be continued.

  • Trio: The Queen Bee Trio — with Kuromi & Cinnamoroll
  • Ex: Pochacco — broke up with him, but she's definitely not over it and still wants him back
  • Personality: Sweet on the surface, calculating underneath
  • Favorite subject: Fashion Club

Kuromi

Senior · Sanrio High's Rebellious Trendsetter

Kuromi is the most fearless girl at Sanrio High — sharp-tongued, mischievous, and completely unbothered by what anyone thinks of her. She lives in Y2K fashion, stays on top of every K-pop release, and always has a comeback ready before you finish your sentence. She and Badtz-Maru are constantly roasting each other and pulling pranks, and it somehow works perfectly. Don't mistake her edge for coldness — if someone messes with one of her people, she is the first one stepping forward.

  • Trio: The Queen Bee Trio — with My Melody & Cinnamoroll
  • Dating: Badtz-Maru
  • Personality: Sharp-tongued, mischievous, fiercely loyal
  • Favorite subject: Fashion Club

Cinnamoroll

Art Club Star · Pompompurin's Best Friend

Cinnamoroll is dreamy, gentle, and always seems slightly elsewhere — but in the best way. Her art pieces always end up on the school bulletin board, and she blushes every single time someone points it out. Her and Pompompurin are inseparable in the way only true best friends can be.

  • Trio: The Queen Bee Trio — with My Melody & Kuromi
  • Best friend: Pompompurin
  • Personality: Dreamy, gentle, creative — goes along with the trio for the attention, not out of cruelty
  • Favorite subject: Baking Club
Z z z

Pompompurin

Pochacco's Circle · Cinnamoroll's Best Friend

Laid-back and lovable, Pompompurin somehow always ends up napping at the exact moment he's needed — yet always shows up for his friends when it counts. He's part of Pochacco's main circle alongside Badtz-Maru and Keroppi, and best friends with Cinnamoroll in the way that just makes sense. He's dating Macaroon, who keeps him on his toes.

  • Circle: Pochacco's crew — with Badtz-Maru & Keroppi
  • Best friend: Cinnamoroll
  • Dating: Macaroon
  • Personality: Chill, loyal, always somehow asleep
  • Favorite subject: Baking Club

Keroppi

Science Club · Pochacco's Circle

Energetic and curious, Keroppi is the first one to raise his hand in class and the last one to leave the science lab. His enthusiasm is completely contagious. He's part of Pochacco's main circle and somehow manages to be friends with absolutely everyone at Sanrio High.

  • Circle: Pochacco, Badtz-Maru, Pompompurin
  • Personality: Energetic, friendly, genuinely kind — the one who actually checks if you're okay
  • Known for: Helping others without being asked, the person everyone goes to when they need someone in their corner
  • Favorite subject: Culture of Cute History

Wish Me Mell

Greenhouse Club · Top of Her Class

Wish Me Mell is one of the smartest students at Sanrio High — the kind of girl who studies ahead, takes perfect notes, and still finds time to tend the greenhouse before first period. She's mostly soft-spoken and focused, but has a bubbly side that comes out around the people she trusts. Her biggest weakness? Mocha — the school heartthrob who drives her absolutely crazy, and she wouldn't have it any other way.

  • Circle: Marroncream, My Sweet Piano — quiet afternoons in the greenhouse or tea parties between classes
  • Dating: Mocha — she fell first and fell hardest; she sees through his arrogance and quietly refuses to let him get away with it
  • Personality: Hardworking, quietly driven, sometimes bubbly — drops everything for the people she loves, but do not let her grade slip
  • Favorite subject: Culture of Cute History

Tuxedosam

Drama Club Star · The Fabulous One

Tuxedosam is the most theatrical student at Sanrio High, and he knows it. Whether he's commanding the stage, critiquing someone's outfit mid-hallway, or launching into a full performance to make a point, Sam does nothing quietly. He's mastered five accents, has an opinion on every fit, and somehow still manages to be friends with absolutely everyone — because underneath the drama, he's completely genuine.

  • Circle: Friends with everyone — especially My Melody, who is the only one who can calm him down mid-monologue
  • Known for: Five-accent repertoire, unsolicited fashion critiques that are somehow always right, and making any situation a performance
  • Personality: Hardworking, over the top, loud, funny, and dramatically fabulous — takes his craft seriously even when no one else does
  • Favorite subject: Drama Club

Cogimyun

Magical Girl · The Enchanting One

Cogimyun looks like the sweetest, softest thing to ever walk the halls of Sanrio High — and she is — but don't let that fool you. She is a magical girl with a television career, a wand collection, and a level of savagery that surprises everyone who underestimates her. She's technically supposed to be saving the world on weekends, but she keeps finding reasons to stick around school and help people instead. She loves it here.

  • Known for: Her fluffy cloud-like presence, rhinestone everything, magic that looks effortless because it is — and the occasional comment that leaves everyone speechless
  • Circle: Makes friends instantly; everyone wants to be near her even if they can't explain why
  • Personality: Cute, magical, surprisingly savage, loves reading, deeply caring — somehow ageless even though she is very much a teenager
  • Favorite subject: Cloud Chemistry

Mocha

Campus Heartthrob · The Effortlessly Cool One

Mocha is the guy everyone notices without him even trying. He's laid-back, athletic, and way too comfortable coasting — whether that's on the field, in the halls, or mid-scroll on his phone. He's not exactly motivated to do much, but he's got Wish Me Mell in his corner, who is somehow the one person who actually gets through to him. They're not officially together. Everyone knows they basically are.

  • Circle: Always seen with his dog (who rocks shades better than anyone), and low-key inseparable from Wish Me Mell
  • Known for: His hair, his sunglasses collection, and doing the absolute minimum while still somehow looking good doing it
  • Personality: Chill, athletic, effortlessly cool — not great at school, but working on actually caring (Wish Me Mell's influence)
  • Favorite subject: Champion Club

Macaroon

Cheer Team · Field Hockey Tomboy

Macaroon is a familiar face around Sanrio High — a cheerleader with a tomboy streak who somehow balances pom-poms and a field hockey stick without missing a beat. She's a fixture of the girl group scene, close with My Melody and Marroncream, and always down for a snowboarding trip. She'll spend her whole allowance on macaroons without a second thought, and she's dating Pompompurin.

  • Best friends: My Melody, Marroncream
  • Dating: Pompompurin
  • Loves: Snowboarding, macaroons, purses, field hockey
  • Personality: Cutesy, tomboyish, always surrounded by friends
  • Favorite subjects: Math Batsu, Field Hockey

Baku

Kuromi 5 · Rebellious Anti-Hero

Baku is the school's resident anti-hero — casual, arrogant, and always working an angle. He's constantly making deals, scamming people out of their money or their homework, and never misses a chance to call someone "sucka." He's part of the Kuromi 5, where he flirts shamelessly with Kuromi because, as he puts it, she's pretty and she's the leader. He loves spray painting, messing with others, and bragging to his friends about how he single-handedly saved the school — whether anyone believes him or not.

  • Group: Kuromi 5
  • Best friend: Berry (who he constantly tells to shut up)
  • Loves: Spray painting, scamming others, messing with people
  • Personality: Rebellious, arrogant, casual, a scammer at heart
  • Favorite subject: None

Berry

Kuromi 5 · Baku's Biggest Fan

Berry is a punk at heart and Baku's self-appointed best friend — whether Baku likes it or not (he doesn't). Berry idolizes him completely, never stops talking, and has zero concept of personal space, hugging Baku at every opportunity he gets. He runs his own magic show in the auditorium, loves pulling pranks, and is a proud member of the Kuromi 5. He's also been secretly keeping a time capsule full of Baku memorabilia, which disturbs Baku deeply every time it comes up. He's dating Cherry, who he adores completely — though she's not afraid to slap him when he starts adoring Baku more than her.

  • Group: Kuromi 5
  • Best friend: Baku (who can't stand him, though Berry hasn't noticed)
  • Dating: Cherry
  • Loves: Pranks, magic, running his magic show in the auditorium
  • Personality: Punk, talkative, clingy, idolizes Baku
  • Favorite subject: Whimsical Club

Cherry

Kuromi 5 · The School's Resident Witch

Cherry is a witch, through and through — she brews her own potions, has taught herself teleportation and floating, and has a creepy little giggle that makes people nervous for good reason. She's the least known member of the Kuromi 5, but the fans who do know her love her the most. She practically lives in the library to skip class, and has claimed the Dragon Domain Ruins section as her own personal hideout. She loves motorcycles and reading, is dating Berry, and doesn't hesitate to slap people when she's bored.

  • Group: Kuromi 5
  • Dating: Berry
  • Friends: Kuromi, Nyanmi, Baku, Berry, Wanmi, Konmi
  • Loves: Motorcycles, reading, potions, the library's Dragon Domain Ruins
  • Skills: Teleportation, floating, a creepy giggle
  • Personality: Mysterious, witchy, easily bored, slaps first and asks questions never

Pandapple

The Peaceful Panda Lover

Pandapple is one of the gentlest souls at Sanrio High. He's rarely seen without his beloved panda, Bobo, and he'd rather spend an afternoon apple picking in the orchards than anywhere else. He loves the meadows, gets lost in a good book, and is happiest baking or cooking up something with apples. He's friends with Keroppi and has had a quiet crush on Hello Kitty for a while now, though he'd never admit it outright.

  • Companion: His panda, Bobo
  • Friends: Keroppi
  • Crush: Hello Kitty
  • Loves: Apple picking, orchards, meadows, reading, baking with apples
  • Personality: Peaceful, kind, loyal
  • Favorite subject: Culture of Cute History
  • Most famous cut line: "Hi, I am Pandapple, you know me. And my fun fact is I am side character #1."

Buppi

The Underrated Soccer Lover

Buppi is a friendly, down-to-earth guy who lives and breathes soccer — he just wishes more people at school were as invested in it as he is. He's kind through and through, the kind of person who'll hand over his own jacket the second he notices someone's cold, which is exactly what he did for My Sweet Piano, who's had a huge crush on him ever since. The two are now dating. He's underrated around school, but anyone who actually knows him knows he's a great guy at heart. He loves fish and seafood dishes, is naturally athletic, and is close friends with Keroppi, Dear Daniel, and Pandapple.

  • Dating: My Sweet Piano
  • Friends: Keroppi, Dear Daniel, Pandapple
  • Loves: Soccer, fish and seafood dishes
  • Personality: Friendly, kind, athletic, underrated

Retsuko

Transfer Student · Student Council

Retsuko transferred to Sanrio High from Aggretkotsu Academy, and she's quickly become known as fast, kind, smart, and a dedicated member of the student council. Her past wasn't easy — she was bullied, and it was her friends Haida and Fenneko who helped her through it. She still carries a temper and once battled real depression, but she's learned to keep both in check. One of her favorite outlets is death metal karaoke. She had a long-running rivalry with Hello Kitty, the two of them framed as rival faces of their story, which eventually built into a full competition — but the rivalry turned into friendship, and Retsuko invited Haida and Fenneko to Sanrio High, who arrived on a flying vehicle. These days, all Retsuko really wants is a relaxing vacation. She loves pizza and strawberry-drizzled milkshake ice cream, enjoys typing and reading newspapers, and is dating Haida — a relationship that eventually leads to marriage once they're grown up.

  • Dating: Haida (they later marry as adults)
  • Friends: Haida, Fenneko
  • Rivalry-turned-friendship: Hello Kitty
  • Loves: Pizza, strawberry-drizzled milkshake ice cream, death metal karaoke, typing, reading newspapers
  • Personality: Fast, kind, smart, has a temper she keeps in check
