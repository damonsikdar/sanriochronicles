The Characters of Sanrio High

Get to know everyone at the most kawaii school in the world.

Hello Kitty

New Transfer Student · The Heart of the Group

Hello Kitty just transferred to Sanrio High alongside her lifelong best friends My Sweet Piano and Dear Daniel. Adjusting hasn't been easy — she's got a temper that flares and a roar that surprises people. But underneath all of that is one of the kindest hearts you'll ever find. She's the glue that holds her whole crew together, even when she doesn't realize it.

  • Best friends: My Sweet Piano, Dear Daniel
  • Love story: Started with a crush on Pochacco — slowly realized her true feelings were for Dear Daniel all along
  • Personality: Fiery, loyal, surprisingly tender-hearted
  • Favorite subject: Math Batsu

My Sweet Piano

Hello Kitty's Best Friend · The Energetic One

My Sweet Piano has been Hello Kitty's best friend since first grade. She is endlessly cheerful, talks to almost everyone she meets, and can get excited over almost anything. She had a habit of making friends within minutes, even if she had just met someone. And yes — she will forget her water bottle every single day.

  • Best friend: Hello Kitty
  • Known for: Making friends within minutes of meeting someone
  • Personality: Energetic, crafty, warm, a little chaotic
  • Favorite subject: Art

Dear Daniel

Hello Kitty's Best Friend · The Calm One

Dear Daniel has been Hello Kitty's best friend since middle school. He's calm, easygoing, and usually the one keeping everyone on schedule. Unlike Piano, Daniel never tries too hard to impress anyone. He showed up on the first day of Sanrio High in his baby blue compact car, ready to drive the group to wherever the story was headed.

  • Best friend: Hello Kitty
  • Known for: Always the calm one keeping the group on schedule
  • Personality: Calm, dependable, charming, shy
  • Favorite subject: Math Batsu

Pochacco

Soccer Team Captain · School Heartthrob

Pochacco is the chill athletic heartthrob of Sanrio High — the kind of person everyone notices the moment he walks into a room. He's My Melody's ex, which makes things complicated now that Hello Kitty is on the scene. Hello Kitty caught a glimpse of him on her first day and couldn't look away. He's interested in her too. My Melody noticed.

  • Circle: Badtz-Maru, Keroppi, Pompompurin
  • Known for: School heartthrob and soccer team captain — everyone notices when he walks in
  • Personality: Chill, athletic, quietly charming
  • Favorite subject: All Sports

Badtz-Maru

Senior · Pochacco's Right-Hand Man

Badtz-Maru is one of the coolest guys at Sanrio High — confident, funny, and effortlessly popular without even trying. He's sassy, witty, and sometimes completely chaotic, always ready with a sarcastic comment before you even finish talking. Don't let the attitude fool you though. The moment someone outside the group gives his friends a hard time, he's the first one to step forward. He acts tough, but he's fiercely loyal.

  • Circle: Pochacco's right-hand man ("Bro #2") — with Keroppi & Pompompurin
  • Dating: Kuromi — regulars at the Haunted Diner, constantly roasting each other
  • Personality: Sassy, witty, laid-back, fiercely loyal
  • Favorite subject: All Sports

My Melody

School Queen Bee · Pochacco's Ex

My Melody is the queen bee of Sanrio High — polished, popular, and not used to losing anything she considers hers. She broke up with Pochacco, but she definitely noticed when he started paying attention to the new transfer student. Her response to finding Hello Kitty's open locker on the first day? A quiet "Oh...nothing." To be continued.

  • Trio: The Queen Bee Trio — with Kuromi & Cinnamoroll
  • Ex: Pochacco — broke up with him, but she's definitely not over it and still wants him back
  • Personality: Sweet on the surface, calculating underneath
  • Favorite subject: Fashion Club

Kuromi

Senior · Sanrio High's Rebellious Trendsetter

Kuromi is the most fearless girl at Sanrio High — sharp-tongued, mischievous, and completely unbothered by what anyone thinks of her. She lives in Y2K fashion, stays on top of every K-pop release, and always has a comeback ready before you finish your sentence. She and Badtz-Maru are constantly roasting each other and pulling pranks, and it somehow works perfectly. Don't mistake her edge for coldness — if someone messes with one of her people, she is the first one stepping forward.

  • Trio: The Queen Bee Trio — with My Melody & Cinnamoroll
  • Dating: Badtz-Maru
  • Personality: Sharp-tongued, mischievous, fiercely loyal
  • Favorite subject: Fashion Club

Cinnamoroll

Art Club Star · Pompompurin's Best Friend

Cinnamoroll is dreamy, gentle, and always seems slightly elsewhere — but in the best way. Her art pieces always end up on the school bulletin board, and she blushes every single time someone points it out. Her and Pompompurin are inseparable in the way only true best friends can be.

  • Trio: The Queen Bee Trio — with My Melody & Kuromi
  • Best friend: Pompompurin
  • Personality: Dreamy, gentle, creative — goes along with the trio for the attention, not out of cruelty
  • Favorite subject: Baking Club
Z z z

Pompompurin

Pochacco's Circle · Cinnamoroll's Best Friend

Laid-back and lovable, Pompompurin somehow always ends up napping at the exact moment he's needed — yet always shows up for his friends when it counts. He's part of Pochacco's main circle alongside Badtz-Maru and Keroppi, and best friends with Cinnamoroll in the way that just makes sense.

  • Circle: Pochacco's crew — with Badtz-Maru & Keroppi
  • Best friend: Cinnamoroll
  • Personality: Chill, loyal, always somehow asleep
  • Favorite subject: Baking Club

Keroppi

Science Club · Pochacco's Circle

Energetic and curious, Keroppi is the first one to raise his hand in class and the last one to leave the science lab. His enthusiasm is completely contagious. He's part of Pochacco's main circle and somehow manages to be friends with absolutely everyone at Sanrio High.

  • Circle: Pochacco, Badtz-Maru, Pompompurin
  • Personality: Energetic, friendly, genuinely kind — the one who actually checks if you're okay
  • Known for: Helping others without being asked, the person everyone goes to when they need someone in their corner
  • Favorite subject: Culture of Cute History

Wish Me Mell

Greenhouse Club · Top of Her Class

Wish Me Mell is one of the smartest students at Sanrio High — the kind of girl who studies ahead, takes perfect notes, and still finds time to tend the greenhouse before first period. She's mostly soft-spoken and focused, but has a bubbly side that comes out around the people she trusts. Her biggest weakness? Mocha — the school heartthrob who drives her absolutely crazy, and she wouldn't have it any other way.

  • Circle: Marroncream, My Sweet Piano — quiet afternoons in the greenhouse or tea parties between classes
  • Dating: Mocha — she fell first and fell hardest; she sees through his arrogance and quietly refuses to let him get away with it
  • Personality: Hardworking, quietly driven, sometimes bubbly — drops everything for the people she loves, but do not let her grade slip
  • Favorite subject: Culture of Cute History

Tuxedosam

Drama Club Star · The Fabulous One

Tuxedosam is the most theatrical student at Sanrio High, and he knows it. Whether he's commanding the stage, critiquing someone's outfit mid-hallway, or launching into a full performance to make a point, Sam does nothing quietly. He's mastered five accents, has an opinion on every fit, and somehow still manages to be friends with absolutely everyone — because underneath the drama, he's completely genuine.

  • Circle: Friends with everyone — especially My Melody, who is the only one who can calm him down mid-monologue
  • Known for: Five-accent repertoire, unsolicited fashion critiques that are somehow always right, and making any situation a performance
  • Personality: Hardworking, over the top, loud, funny, and dramatically fabulous — takes his craft seriously even when no one else does
  • Favorite subject: Drama Club
